MASTER RESUME TEMPLATE INSTRUCTIONS
====================================

Your master resume template should be a Word document (.docx) saved as:
  master-resume-template.docx

This file will be used to automatically generate tailored resumes for each job.

TEMPLATE VARIABLES
==================

You can use these variables anywhere in your resume. They will be replaced
automatically when generating a resume for a specific job:

{targetCompany}    - The company name (e.g., "Google", "Microsoft")
{targetRole}       - The job title (e.g., "Senior Software Engineer")
{targetLocation}   - The location (e.g., "Remote", "San Francisco, CA")
{skills}           - Key skills for the job, comma-separated
{date}             - Current date in "Month DD, YYYY" format

EXAMPLE USAGE
=============

Cover Letter Section:
---------------------
Dear Hiring Manager,

I am excited to apply for the {targetRole} position at {targetCompany}.
With over 12 years of experience in software engineering and platform
leadership, I am confident I can make an immediate impact on your team
in {targetLocation}.


Objective Statement:
-------------------
Seeking a {targetRole} role at {targetCompany} where I can leverage my
expertise in {skills} to drive innovation and technical excellence.


Header:
-------
John Doe
Senior Engineering Leader
Application for {targetRole} at {targetCompany}
{date}


TIPS
====

1. Keep your core content (experience, education) as-is
2. Use variables in:
   - Cover letter
   - Objective/summary
   - Header/footer
   - Any customizable sections

3. Test your template by:
   - Adding a job in the tracker
   - Clicking "Generate Resume"
   - Checking if variables were replaced correctly

4. You can have multiple versions if needed:
   - master-resume-template.docx (main)
   - master-resume-technical.docx (for technical roles)
   - master-resume-leadership.docx (for management roles)

5. To use alternate templates:
   - Edit server/resume/generator.js
   - Change the templatePath based on job type/company

EXAMPLE TEMPLATE
================

Create a Word document with something like this:

-------------------------------------------

[YOUR NAME]
[Contact Information]

Application for {targetRole} at {targetCompany}
{date}

PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY
I am a seasoned engineering leader with 12+ years of experience seeking
to join {targetCompany} as a {targetRole}. My expertise in {skills}
aligns perfectly with your team's needs.

EXPERIENCE
[Your work history - keep this the same]

EDUCATION
[Your education - keep this the same]

SKILLS
• {skills}
• [Your other skills]

-------------------------------------------

Save this as master-resume-template.docx in the data/ folder.

Now when you generate a resume for a "Senior Backend Engineer" position
at "Stripe", it will automatically fill in:
- {targetCompany} → Stripe
- {targetRole} → Senior Backend Engineer
- {skills} → Python, REST APIs, PostgreSQL, Kubernetes, etc.
- {date} → March 9, 2026
